VOX TEMPLI · PUBLIC

This page is the static dashboard. It reads the public mempool.space API. It does not see your node, your peers, or your cookie.

Space or the corner label — sound on/off. Rhythm follows mempool load. Pitch follows the fee.

Lightning — a transaction mempool.space just reported. Size follows its vbytes. Color follows its sat/vB, green when it is cheap and orange when it is not.

Ring of light — a block was found.

Price — the USD number mempool.space publishes. It is not UTXOracle. The appliance on your own node is the one that prices from the UTXO set.

Flags — feed down, live socket down, an old tip, a heavy mempool, or a high fast fee. That is the public network and this data source, not a diagnosis of a machine you run.

H closes this panel.
